## The rule

> If we cannot show the source, we do not put it on the page.

That applies to stars, users, revenue, replies, partnerships and results. This repo is the working plan for [FastPress Labs](https://github.com/fastpresspages): what we are building, who we are asking, what we send, and how we measure whether it worked.

It is public because the plan is not the moat. The work is.

## The 90-day bet

LaunchGrade is the flagship: a free, zero-dependency CLI that grades a launch page across 53 checks. The bet is simple:

1. **Give away the diagnosis.** The scan, the checklist, the reports and the source are free.
2. **Earn the conversation.** A specific, useful audit is a better opener than a cold pitch.
3. **Sell the fix only when asked.** Paid work is scoped in writing. No retainer traps, no fake urgency.
4. **Publish the receipts.** Before/after reports, dates, tests, changelog. Let people check the work.

Success is not "going viral". It is ten real audits, three collaboration conversations and one repeatable paid offer that does not require pretending.
